Behavior

Where the cat may go and what it may climb.
Stay on top of fullscreen apps
Recommended — the cat claims the whole screen.
Walk & jump on window tops
The cat strolls and hops along the top border of normal (resized) windows. Maximized full-screen windows are skipped, and it rides along when you move or resize a window it stands on. Off saves CPU.
Start with Windows
No-walk zones
Areas the cat refuses to enter — and window tops it won't land on. Select them straight off the screen, like a Windows screenshot.
Select an area
A fullscreen crosshair overlay opens — drag a rectangle, Esc cancels. Works across all monitors.

Sounds

Recorded from real, opinionated cats.
All sounds (master)
One switch for everything: meows, purrs, toys, gags, chimes. Off = a completely silent cat.
Single-click meow
One quick tap on the cat plays one natural single meow. (Needs All sounds on.)
Double-click meow
A double-click plays the classic meow — and nothing else (no window opens). (Needs All sounds on.)
Random meows
At random times the cat meows on its own — usually one natural meow, sometimes a burst of several. Never plays while a click meow is sounding. (Needs All sounds on.)
Contextual sounds
Toy squeaks on jumps, coins, achievement chimes, purrs from petting, build reactions. (Needs All sounds on.)
Reminder chime
The little chirp when a reminder fires — the message bubble itself is unaffected. (Needs All sounds on.)
Footstep pattering is gone — the cat walks in silence (v3.12). Quick tap = one natural single meow · double-click = the classic meow · pet (hold) = purring.
